# Member Guide

## Start here

Use this site for ordinary church life: reading Scripture, seeing what is relevant today, joining prayer, reading announcements, checking church gatherings, browsing community activities, and keeping your profile current.

Your usual first page is Today. Today is an agenda and action page. It is not a complete list of everything in the system.

## Common tasks

1. Open Today.
2. Review today's reading, visible Bible Study meetings, visible Church Gatherings, activity reminders, and any personal serving action that has been explicitly assigned to you.
3. Open the linked item when you need the full detail.
4. Use the owning page when you need a complete list: Reading, Bible Study, Prayer, Church Gatherings, Activities, Announcements, Calendar, My Serving, or Profile.

## What you can do

- Use Reading to follow Bible reading plans, open passages, and check in.
- Use Bible Study to see meetings available to you and open their preparation details.
- Use Prayer to read, post, update, and respond to prayer requests according to the page rules.
- Use Calendar to see a read-only date view of church items visible to you.
- Use Church Gatherings for official gatherings you are allowed to see.
- Use Activities for community or fellowship activities and signup intent.
- Use Announcements for official church communication.
- Use Profile to maintain your account basics and language preference.

## How to do it

### Today

Open Today when you want the shortest path to what needs attention now. It may show reading, gatherings, Bible Study, activity reminders, announcements, and personal serving items. Open each link for the full page.

### Reading

Open Reading, choose the current plan, read the passage, and use the check-in action when you are done. If Today links to a reading item, it takes you back to the same reading workflow.

### Bible Study

Open Bible Study and choose a visible meeting. Read the meeting details and preparation content that is available. Seeing a Bible Study meeting means it is visible to you; it does not automatically mean you have a serving role for that meeting.

### Prayer

Open Prayer, choose a request, and use the available action such as prayed, comment, edit, or report. Follow the privacy and visibility shown on the page.

### Calendar

Open Calendar and choose a month or day. Calendar is read-only. It does not publish, edit, assign, confirm, or cancel anything.

### Church Gatherings

Open Church Gatherings and choose a visible gathering. Seeing a gathering means it is visible to you; it does not mean you are assigned to serve.

### Activities

Open Activities and choose a published activity that is visible to you. Sign up only when you plan to attend. Signup is attendance intent, not serving.

Before the activity starts, you may cancel your own active signup while the activity is still published. Cancellation is immediate and does not need organizer or staff approval. The system keeps the signup history, but your cancellation frees the spot for capacity.

You may sign up again before the activity starts if the activity is still eligible for you and capacity is available. If the activity is full, a new signup or re-signup is not available. After the activity starts, ordinary signup changes are frozen. There is no waitlist in the current Activities version.

### Announcements

Open Announcements to read current official communication visible to you. Important announcements may also appear on Today.

### Profile / account menu

Open your account menu and choose Profile to update available account fields. The account menu also contains Help Center, language options, and My Units when that surface is available to you.

## What this does not mean

- Seeing an event does not make you a server.
- Seeing a Bible Study meeting does not make you a Bible Study role holder.
- Belonging to a church structure unit does not grant leadership, staff access, or serving.
- Activity signup is attendance intent only.
- Calendar is read-only.
- Today is an agenda, not the full serving workspace.

## Troubleshooting / where to go next

If an item you expected is missing, check whether it is on the owning page and whether it is meant for your audience. If you believe your membership, visibility, or account language is wrong, contact a church staff user.
