# Church Structure / Group Leader Guide

## Start here

Church Structure describes ordinary belonging and care context. It does not automatically grant leadership, staff access, serving, or management authority.

Use My Units only when it appears for your account. That means the system already has an explicit path that makes at least one unit manageable for you.

## Common tasks

1. Open your account menu.
2. Choose My Units when it is available.
3. Select a unit you manage.
4. Review the member and coworker information shown on that unit page.
5. Use the available add, end, approve, reject, or record actions only when the page offers them.

## What you can do

- Review units that you are allowed to manage.
- Maintain small-group belonging where the current workflow permits it.
- Review and manage pending requests for the small-group units you manage.
- Ask church staff to use the broader structure setup and review pages when a change is outside your delegated surface.

## How to do it

### Review a unit

Open My Units and choose the unit. Check the unit path, current members, and coworker information before making changes.

### Add a member

Use the add-member action only for an eligible unassigned user. The page blocks users who already have an active primary belonging record.

### End a membership

Use the end action on the unit page when a member's current belonging should end. This ends belonging; it does not delete the user account.

### Membership requests

A delegated group leader reviews pending requests on the managed small-group page under My Units. Approve or reject actions appear there only when you have the applicable delegated management access.

Approve only when the requested small group is correct and the user does not already have another active primary belonging record. Existing active-primary conflicts stay blocked. This guide does not imply a transfer workflow.

Staff may separately use the global Staff membership-request queue.

### Coworker / member information

Use the information shown on the unit page for care and maintenance. Do not treat a member list as a serving roster or permission list.

## What this does not mean

- Membership is not leadership.
- Membership is not serving.
- A structure unit is not a ministry team assignment.
- My Units belonging maintenance does not create serving assignments, Bible Study roles, or staff permissions.
- Do not create a fake Unassigned unit. Unassigned is a candidate state, not a Church Structure unit.

## Troubleshooting / where to go next

If My Units is missing, you do not currently have that delegated surface. If a member cannot be added or approved, check for an existing active primary belonging record or ask staff to review the structure data.
