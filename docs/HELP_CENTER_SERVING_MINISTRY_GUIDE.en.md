# Serving / Ministry Guide

## Start here

Use My Serving when you have explicit serving work. Serving is not inferred from membership, audience visibility, staff status, or event visibility.

My Serving is a personal serving workspace. It helps you see your own assigned serving, confirm when confirmation is available, and follow links to the related gathering, team, or Bible Study meeting.

## Common tasks

1. Open My Serving.
2. Review items that need attention, today's serving, this week's serving, later serving, and past serving.
3. Open a serving card for the related Church Gathering or Bible Study context.
4. Confirm the assignment when the page offers a confirmation action.
5. Use staff or team management pages only when you already have the appropriate permission.

## What you can do

- See your own explicit Church Gathering serving assignments.
- See Bible Study serving that is explicitly linked to your user account.
- Confirm serving when the current workflow supports confirmation.
- Follow links to the related gathering, meeting, team, or assignment context.
- If you manage a team or have staff access, use the existing management surfaces for scheduling and review.

## How to do it

### My Serving

Open My Serving from the main navigation when it is available. Start with items needing attention, then review today's and upcoming serving. Use the past tab when you need older records.

### Confirmation

If a serving card offers a confirmation action, review the date, time, team, assignment status, and notes before confirming. Confirmation belongs to My Serving; Calendar and Today may link you there, but they do not confirm for you.

### Church Gathering serving

For a Church Gathering serving assignment, open the serving card and check the related gathering context. Seeing the gathering as a member is separate from being assigned to serve at it.

### Bible Study serving

For Bible Study serving, open the role card in My Serving. A Bible Study role appears as your personal serving only when it is linked to your user account.

### Leader / staff attention

For scheduling or review work, follow the link to the owning management page. My Serving summarizes attention; it does not replace the scheduling workflow.

## What this does not mean

- Church structure membership is belonging, not serving.
- Audience visibility is not serving.
- Event visibility is not serving.
- A display name on a Bible Study role does not create your personal serving item unless the role is linked to your user.
- A long-term role or team responsibility is not the same as a weekly event-serving assignment.
- My Serving requires explicit serving data.

## Troubleshooting / where to go next

If you expect to see a serving item but it is missing, ask the scheduling staff to check whether your user account is explicitly linked to the assignment or Bible Study role. Do not create a membership or audience workaround to make serving appear.
